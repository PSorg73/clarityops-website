Privacy notice

This notice explains how ClarityOps Systems handles information submitted through this website.

Information collected

If you use the contact form, you provide your name, email address, and message. Please do not include confidential, sensitive, or regulated information in the form.

How it is used

The information is used to respond to your inquiry, understand what help you are seeking, and communicate about a possible working relationship. ClarityOps Systems does not sell your personal information.

Service providers

The website is hosted by Netlify. Contact-form submissions are processed by Formspree. Appointment scheduling is offered through an external Calendly link. These providers may process information under their own privacy policies.

Retention and security

Inquiry information is kept only as long as reasonably needed for communication, business records, and applicable obligations. Reasonable care is used to protect it, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may ask what personal information ClarityOps Systems holds about you, request a correction, or ask that it be deleted when no legal or business-record obligation requires retention.
